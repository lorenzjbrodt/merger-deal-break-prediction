# Benchmark table

Train: announcements 2001-2018. Test: 2019-2025. Brackets are bootstrap 95% confidence intervals (2000 resamples of test deals). Hyper-parameters are chosen by expanding-window CV inside the training years.

Samples: the sample as specified has 4,744 deals. 276 of them are reverse mergers (de-SPACs and shell mergers), 275 of which fall in the test period and account for 52 test-period breaks; the training period has almost none. The **core sample** (4,468 deals) excludes them and is the primary sample. Targets: **adverse break** = cancelled for a reason other than a superior competing bid (primary); **any break** = cancelled.
